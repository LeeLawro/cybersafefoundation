Why we exist

Online safety should not depend on your budget

The charities and community groups who look after the most vulnerable people are usually the ones with the least money for digital safety. They hold sensitive information about people who cannot afford for it to leak, and they run on volunteers, shared laptops and goodwill.

Commercial cyber security is priced for organisations that have a budget line for it. Most small charities do not. That gap is the reason the Foundation exists.

We pay for those organisations to have the same online safety training and tools a well funded organisation would have. Not a cut down version, the same thing.

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17106862, registered office 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. A community interest company is a UK legal form for social enterprises, regulated by the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies and subject to a statutory asset lock. The CyberSafe Coach platform is built and operated by CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd (company number 16872486); the Foundation is a separate company that funds access to it for organisations that cannot pay.
