What we do

The charities and community groups who look after the most vulnerable people are usually the ones with the least money for digital safety. They hold sensitive information about people who cannot afford for it to leak, and they run on volunteers, shared laptops and goodwill.

We exist to close that gap. The Foundation funds full access to the CyberSafe Coach online safety platform for organisations that could not otherwise pay for it, so their staff and volunteers get the same training a well funded organisation would.

Who can apply for funded access

To receive funded access through the Foundation, your organisation needs to meet all of the following.

  • Registered with the Charity Commission for England and Wales, OSCR in Scotland, or CCNI in Northern Ireland.
  • Based in the UK.
  • Annual income below £100,000, or evidence that the standard rate is a genuine barrier.
  • Using it for staff, volunteers or the people you support, not for commercial purposes.
  • Willing to send us a short impact report after six months and at each annual renewal.

How the process works

  1. ApplyVerify your charity number and complete a short form. Around 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. We verifyWe check your registration against the public register for your regulator.
  3. We assessWe check the funds the Foundation currently holds and make a decision.
  4. DecisionA written decision within 10 working days of a complete application.
  5. Access set upYour account is created and login details go to your named contact.
  6. Annual reviewReviewed every 12 months, with a brief impact report.
Being straight with you about funded places

The Foundation can only award what it holds. If we cannot fund you immediately we will say so honestly, tell you your position on the waitlist, and set out the alternatives. We use the waitlist as evidence of demand when we apply for grants, so being on it genuinely helps us fund more organisations. Free access for individuals and families is never affected.

How this is funded

We are open about where the money comes from, because organisations are right to ask. The Foundation is funded in three ways.

One

The standing pledge

2% of every CyberSafe Coach subscription is donated to the Foundation automatically. No opt in, it simply happens.

Two

Optional social impact donation

Subscribers can choose to add a one off 10% social impact donation at the checkout.

Three

Sponsored Access

A business sponsors one named local school, charity or community group for a full year, from £420. The sponsor is publicly recognised and receives a quarterly impact report.
