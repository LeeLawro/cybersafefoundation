Public bodies and partners

Somewhere free to send people

If you meet small charities and members of the public who need this, here is exactly what is on offer and exactly what is not.

  • No cost to youFunded access is paid for by the Foundation, not by the organisation receiving it.
  • A written decisionWhatever the answer. If we cannot fund you, we say so and tell you why.
  • A regulated entityCommunity interest company 17106862, subject to a statutory asset lock.
  • Two directors, published policiesGovernance, asset lock and access decisions are written down.

Sponsored Access

Ask to be sponsored by a local business

If you cannot fund online safety training yourself, a local business may be able to fund it for you. Tell us about your organisation and we will put you on the list we match sponsors against. It costs you nothing and there is no obligation.

What you get

A full year, paid for

  • Full platform access for your staff and volunteers.
  • Your safety check, action plan and compliance tracking.
  • No cost to your organisation at any point.
What we ask

Very little

  • That your sponsor can be named publicly as having funded you.
  • A short note each quarter on what has changed, so we can report back honestly.

Honest about the waiting

We cannot promise a sponsor. Being on the list means we put you forward when a business asks to fund an organisation like yours, and we will tell you where you stand rather than leaving you wondering.

If you would rather not wait, two other routes exist. You can apply for funded access through the Foundation's own pooled fund, or subscribe directly, which costs less than most small teams expect. If there are five or fewer of you, the Micro plan is cheaper than the charity rate, and Solo is cheaper again if it is just you. See the plans.

Put us forward

Tell us who you are, what you do and roughly how many people are in your team. That is enough to get started.

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17106862, registered office 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. A community interest company is a UK legal form for social enterprises, regulated by the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies and subject to a statutory asset lock. The CyberSafe Coach platform is built and operated by CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd (company number 16872486); the Foundation is a separate company that funds access to it for organisations that cannot pay.
