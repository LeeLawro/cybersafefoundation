Sponsored Access

Pay for one organisation's online safety for a year

You choose a named local school, charity or community group. They get the full platform for a year at no cost to them. You are recognised as their sponsor and you get a quarterly report showing what actually changed.

What they get

The full platform, not a trial

  • Full access for their staff and volunteers.
  • Their safety check, action plan and compliance tracking.
  • A full year, from the day they are set up.
What you get

Something you can actually report on

  • Named public recognition as their sponsor.
  • A quarterly impact report you can use internally or publicly.
  • A named organisation in your own community, not a general fund.

How it works

  1. You tell us roughly what you want to fund and whether you have an organisation in mind.
  2. If you do not, we match you with one from the waitlist and you approve the match.
  3. We invoice you, set them up, and tell them who paid for it.
  4. You get a written impact report every quarter for the year.

Sponsorship starts from £420 for a year. Larger amounts sponsor more organisations or a larger one. We will confirm the exact figure and the invoice details before anything is agreed.

Being straight with you

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company, not a registered charity. That matters for how you treat the payment in your accounts, so we say it plainly rather than letting you find out later. This is sponsorship with recognition, not a charitable donation, and there is no Gift Aid.

We are company 17106862, subject to a statutory asset lock. Access decisions are made against written criteria and cannot be taken to benefit CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd commercially.

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17106862, registered office 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. A community interest company is a UK legal form for social enterprises, regulated by the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies and subject to a statutory asset lock. The CyberSafe Coach platform is built and operated by CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd (company number 16872486); the Foundation is a separate company that funds access to it for organisations that cannot pay.
