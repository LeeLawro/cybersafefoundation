Nonprofits and community organizations

Funded access to the full platform, at no cost to you

Your staff and volunteers get the same online safety training a well funded organization would have. The Foundation pays for it.

  • No cost to youFunded access is paid for by the Foundation, not by the organization receiving it.
  • A written decisionWhatever the answer. If we cannot fund you, we say so and tell you why.
  • A regulated entityCommunity interest company 17106862, subject to a statutory asset lock.

What you get

The full platform, not a trial

  • Full access for your staff and volunteers.

Who can apply

You need to meet all five

  • Recognized by the IRS as a 501(c)(3) tax exempt organization.
  • Based in the United States.
  • Annual income below $130,000, or evidence that the standard rate is a genuine barrier.
  • Using it for staff, volunteers or the people you support, not for commercial purposes.
  • Willing to send a short impact report after six months and at each annual renewal.

Not sure whether you qualify? Ask us before you fill anything in and we will tell you honestly whether it is worth your time.

The process

What happens after you apply

Six steps, and you hear from us in writing at the end of it. No sales call and nobody will ring you to upsell anything.

  1. ApplyEmail us your EIN and IRS determination letter. US applications are reviewed by hand.
  2. We verifyWe verify your 501(c)(3) status against IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search.
  3. We assessWe check the funds the Foundation currently holds and make a decision.
  4. DecisionA written decision, whatever the answer.
  5. Access set upYour account is created and login details go to your named contact.
  6. Annual reviewReviewed every 12 months, with a brief impact report from you.
Where US applications stand

Funded places outside the UK are currently limited, because the fund is raised in the UK. We review every US application and we will tell you honestly where you stand rather than leaving you waiting.

Being straight with you about funded places

The Foundation can only award what it holds. If we cannot fund you immediately we will say so honestly, tell you your position on the waitlist, and set out the alternatives rather than leaving you waiting without an answer.

We use the waitlist as evidence of demand when we apply for grants, so being on it genuinely helps us fund more organizations.

Questions we get asked

Is this genuinely free, or will we be charged later?

Genuinely free. Funded access is paid for by the Foundation. There is no card required, no trial that converts, and no charge at the end of it. Access is reviewed every 12 months and we will tell you in writing if anything changes.

What is the impact report you ask for?

A short written update after six months and at each renewal, telling us how it has been used and what difference it made. Usually a few paragraphs. We need it because a community interest company has to demonstrate community benefit, and because funders ask us for evidence.

Can we share access with the people we support?

Yes, where it is appropriate and safeguarded. What it cannot be used for is commercial or income generating activity.

What if our income is above $130,000?

You can still apply, but you will need to show that the standard rate is a genuine barrier. Email us before applying.

Who actually decides?

The Foundation's directors, against written eligibility criteria and the funds the C.I.C. holds at the time. The decision cannot be taken to benefit CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd commercially. That restriction is written into our access policy and is a requirement of the asset lock.

What happens to our data?

We check your EIN and IRS determination letter against the public register, and we hold the contact details you give us so we can administer your access. We do not sell data and we do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

Ready to apply?

Email us your details and we will review your application by hand.

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17106862, registered office 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. A community interest company is a UK legal form for social enterprises, regulated by the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies and subject to a statutory asset lock. It is not a US 501(c)(3), so contributions are not deductible as charitable donations for US tax purposes. The CyberSafe Coach platform is built and operated by CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd (company number 16872486); the Foundation is a separate company that funds access to it for organizations that cannot pay.
