The short version

What you can safely tell people

If you work with small nonprofits, community groups or members of the public and you want somewhere free to send them, this page tells you exactly what is on offer and exactly what is not, so you are never caught out.

Free at the point of delivery

What you can point people at

  • Recognized nonprofits and community organizations can apply for fully funded access to the platform. No cost to them.
  • Any individual or family can use the free online safety check. No application, no cost, nothing to buy.

Both are paid for by CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C., a not for profit community interest company.

Not free

What you should not point people at

  • Small businesses, schools, care providers and public authorities all pay for the platform commercially.

We say this plainly because public sector partners are often not permitted to advocate a service that carries a fee, and we would rather you heard the boundary from us than found it later.

What we can give your team

All of this is free and there is nothing to sign. We work with law enforcement, city and county agencies, and community partners.

  • A one page leaflet with QR codes, designed to be handed over in person.
  • A short paragraph you can drop into a newsletter, bulletin or briefing.
  • A session for your team, in person or online, explaining what the offer is and who it suits, so your people can answer questions confidently.
  • A named contact who answers emails, so a referral never disappears into a queue.

CyberSafe Foundation C.I.C. is a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company number 17106862, registered office 124 City Road, London EC1V 2NX. A community interest company is a UK legal form for social enterprises, regulated by the Office of the Regulator of Community Interest Companies and subject to a statutory asset lock. It is not a US 501(c)(3), so contributions are not deductible as charitable donations for US tax purposes. The CyberSafe Coach platform is built and operated by CyberSafe Coach Plus Ltd (company number 16872486); the Foundation is a separate company that funds access to it for organizations that cannot pay.
